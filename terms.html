CP : All in One terms of use

Effective October 21, 2026.

CP : All in One is an independent tool for organizing competitive programming practice. It provides contest schedules, optional calendar integration, accepted-solution backups and local problem notes. It is not endorsed by the integrated platforms.

Use only accounts and repositories you own or are authorized to access. Upload only source code you have permission to store. Follow the rules of each programming platform and contest; automatic contest holds do not replace your responsibility to avoid disclosing solutions during a contest.

You retain the rights you have in your notes and source code. Connecting a provider authorizes the operations described in the extension and its privacy policy; it does not transfer ownership of your content to the maintainer. Disconnecting does not erase files or events already created with external providers.

Contest feeds and website layouts can change or become unavailable. Check important dates against the contest organizer. The extension does not guarantee complete contest coverage, uninterrupted availability, successful capture of every submission, or recovery of all historical solutions. Keep independent copies of important work. Local notes may be lost when extension storage is removed.

Google Calendar and GitHub connections are optional and can be revoked through the provider's account settings. Their services have separate terms and policies. Our data practices are described in the privacy policy.
